# Additional scenario art

Ten additional scenes generated for historical scenarios and existing event cards. Source specs: `extra-scenes.json`. The original `prompts.json` and `PROMPTS.md` remain unchanged.

Import from the repo root:

```sh
uvx --with pillow python scripts/pixel-art.py import
```

Historical cards and in-game events use `scenarioImages.ts`; numbered `new_*` events are matched in `selectScene.ts`. Each new scene has animated overlays in `RasterSceneEffects.tsx`.

## scenario_hyperinflation

Size: 1536x864

```text
Authentic 16-bit pixel art in the style of a SNES / Sega Genesis game. Visible square pixels, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, limited palette of about 32 colors, flat colors with 1-pixel dark outlines. Satirical Argentine political video game. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere. Wide 16:9 game scene, readable at small size, strong silhouette, one clear focal point in the center third. Argentina during the 1989 hyperinflation crisis: a crowded small neighborhood grocery, a shopkeeper replacing blank price tags, a family carrying a wheelbarrow of banknotes, empty bread baskets, older 1980s cars visible outside, tense daytime atmosphere.
```
